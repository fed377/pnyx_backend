2. How we use it

  • To operate the core product — computing your grid positions, recommending content, and showing alignment between you and other people
  • To screen content you post for policy violations before it's shown to anyone else
  • To send you push and email notifications you've asked for (votes, replies, alignment matches, password resets)
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, and violations of our Terms of Service
  • To respond to support requests and legal obligations (including copyright notices)
  • To understand how PNYX is used and improve it (aggregate product analytics — see Section 4)

3. Content moderation and AI processing

When you post, the text and media are sent to Google's Gemini API to be scored on PNYX's five value grids and screened for policy violations, before anyone else can see the post. This processing is automated. Google processes this data as our subprocessor and does not use it to train its own models beyond what's disclosed in its own API terms.

4. Who we share data with

We don't sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that help us run PNYX, each acting under contract as our data processor:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage hosting
  • Google (Gemini API, Google Sign-In) — content scoring/moderation, and optional sign-in
  • Expo — push notification delivery
  • Resend — transactional email (password resets, confirmations)
  • PostHog — product analytics (e.g. which screens and features are used, how long onboarding takes). Events never include post/message content or your raw vote history; once you're signed in, PostHog identifies your account only by a one-way hash of your account ID, never the ID itself

Other users can see whatever you've chosen to make visible in your privacy settings: your profile, your public posts, and (unless you've made your account Private) your grid positions and the alignment percentage between you and them. Your email, password, birthday, and notification preferences are never shown to anyone else.

Future aggregated data products

We may in the future offer aggregated, anonymized trend data (for example, "how a demographic segment in a region skews on a given grid") to businesses or researchers. If we build this, no individual will be identifiable in it, cohorts will be sized to prevent re-identification, and we will update this Policy and seek any additional consent required by law before launching it. This Policy does not currently authorize, and no such product exists today.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account ("Forget Me" in Settings), we permanently delete your profile, votes, posts, messages, and uploaded media. Some information may be retained briefly afterward where required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.
